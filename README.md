# rbi_gradeb_descriptiveprep

What this skill includes:
Paper-Specific Frameworks: Standardized structures (Intro-Body-Conclusion) tailored for 10-mark (~400 words) and 15-mark (~600 words) descriptive questions in Economic & Social Issues (ESI) and Finance & Management (FM).
English Writing Skills Guidelines: Specific formatting, word counts, and structural rules for Essay Writing, Precis Writing, and Reading Comprehension.
Open-Source & YouTube Learning Resource References: Direct alignment with key RBI publications (RBI Annual Report, Financial Stability Report, MPC Statements), government sources (Economic Survey, Union Budget), and top free educational video platforms (EduTap, Anuj Jindal, CrackGradeB, ixamBee).
Interactive Evaluation Workflow: Structured rubric scoring, gap analysis, and model answer enhancement suggestions whenever you paste an answer for feedback.
